Earth map data sources

Building footprints and available heights come from © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), in the © OpenMapTiles schema, served by OpenFreeMap. Buildings without measured heights use the source's estimated height, or a 6 m fallback. Roofs are extrusions of the available footprints, not photogrammetry. Facade windows and their illumination are illustrative.

Satellite imagery and elevation: © Mapbox, with the underlying providers credited by Mapbox.

Night-light emission uses the NASA Black Marble 2016 grayscale composite, from Suomi NPP VIIRS data. NASA Earth Observatory / Joshua Stevens; data from Miguel Román, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. The 3 km image was downsampled to 8192 × 4096 for this globe. This is a historical composite, not a live view of power usage.

Cloud coverage uses NASA's Blue Marble (2002) cloud composite. Image: Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; enhancements by Robert Simmon. The 2048 × 1024 image is encoded as a grayscale coverage texture. The historical coverage field seeds procedural cloud volumes for the ten main WMO cloud families. Altitude, wind, density, shadows, and lighting are simulated; this is not live weather.

Cloud rendering references: Guerrilla Games: Nubis; Epic Games: Volumetric Clouds; WMO International Cloud Atlas. Procedural noise and shaders are locally authored approximations.

The building decoder uses vector-tile-js, pbf, and earcut; open-source licenses.

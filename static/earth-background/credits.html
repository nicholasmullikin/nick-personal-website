Earth background imagery

The article background is an orbital view rendered by the same globe, atmosphere and volumetric cloud code as the interactive Earth simulator.

Surface and clouds: NASA Blue Marble. Cloud composite: Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; enhancements by Robert Simmon (2002).

Night lights: NASA Black Marble (2016), Suomi NPP VIIRS. NASA Earth Observatory / Joshua Stevens; data from Miguel Román, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.

These are historical composites. Rotation, cloud drift and illumination are illustrative, not live weather or a real-time solar position.

To conserve energy, phones, reduced-motion settings, data-saving connections and smaller devices receive a still image. Animation on eligible desktops is limited to six frames per second, pauses while scrolling, in hidden tabs, or while viewing interactive demos, and otherwise rotates once every 20 minutes of active animation. A footer control can disable animation.
